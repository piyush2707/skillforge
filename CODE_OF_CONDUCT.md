# Contributor Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in SkillForge a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, communicating constructively, accepting feedback gracefully, and focusing discussions on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
